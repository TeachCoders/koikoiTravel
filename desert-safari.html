Frequently Asked Questions about Desert Safari

Helpful answers to common traveler questions when planning a trip to Desert Safari.

The Sam Sand Dunes and Khuri Sand Dunes in Jaisalmer (Thar Desert, Rajasthan) are the most authentic locations for golden desert safaris. Other notable spots include Osian near Jodhpur and the dunes around Bikaner.
Yes! Modern luxury desert camps offer Swiss-style canvas tents with attached tiled bathrooms, hot water, comfortable double beds, and heavy quilts to keep you warm against crisp winter desert night temperatures.
Yes, experienced desert drivers navigate customized 4x4 vehicles across sand dunes. Gentle scenic drives can be requested for seniors, pregnant women, and small children, while thrill-seekers can opt for high-adrenaline crest surfing.
Pack light, breathable cotton clothes, sunglasses, and sun hats for midday sun. For the evening and night, carry warm jackets, a fleece pullover, and socks, as desert temperatures drop rapidly after sunset.
Evenings come alive around a glowing campfire with live performances by famous Kalbelia gypsy dancers, fire-eaters, and soul-stirring Thar folk songs played on the kamaicha, morchang, and dholak.
A standard luxury package includes a sunset camel ride across sand ripples, 4x4 Jeep dune bashing, traditional Rajasthani dhol and tikka welcome, evening bonfire with Kalbelia folk dance and Manganiyar music, a royal buffet dinner, and an overnight luxury tent stay.
Sam Dunes (42 km from Jaisalmer) features tall shifting dunes and energetic evening entertainment with numerous desert resorts. Khuri Dunes (48 km) is quieter, less crowded, and ideal for travelers seeking serenity, village culture, and stargazing.
October to March is the ideal season. Daytime temperatures are pleasant and sunny (22°C–28°C), while nights are cool and refreshing (8°C–14°C). Desert safaris are generally closed or avoided during scorching summer months (April to June).
Yes, reputable desert camps serve generous buffet spreads with vegetarian and non-vegetarian options, including authentic Rajasthani specialties like Dal Baati Churma and Gatte ki Sabzi, alongside mild continental dishes.
Yes, most travelers spend one day touring Jaisalmer's living Golden Fort, Jain temples, Patwon ki Haveli, and Gadisar Lake, before heading to the sand dunes for the afternoon safari and overnight camp stay.

Everything About Desert Safari

The Thar Desert of Rajasthan is a mesmerizing realm of shifting golden dunes, vibrant royal traditions, and starlit night skies. Our desert safari and luxury camping tour packages take you into the very soul of the desert, from the golden sandstone fortress of Jaisalmer and the dunes of Sam and Khuri to the royal oasis of Bikaner and the camel fair traditions of Pushkar.

Ride across sunset dunes on a gentle camel trek or an exhilarating 4x4 dune-bashing excursion, followed by an evening around a crackling campfire featuring vibrant Rajasthani folk dance, Kalbelia performers, and authentic desert cuisine under the stars. Stay in handcrafted Swiss desert tents equipped with modern amenities and attached baths, and explore ancient desert cenotaphs and forgotten ghost villages with our expert local chauffeurs and guides.
