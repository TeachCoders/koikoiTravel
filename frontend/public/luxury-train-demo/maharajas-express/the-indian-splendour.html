OVERVIEW

About The Indian Splendour

The classic reverse royal tour from Delhi across Agra, Ranthambore, Jaipur, Bikaner sand dunes, Mehrangarh Fort, Lake Pichola, culminating in Mumbai. Seven unforgettable days capturing the grandeur of north and western India in absolute 5-star comfort.
DETAILED PLAN

The Indian Splendour Itinerary

Day 1: Delhi to Agra – Boarding & Royal Welcome

Afternoon: Registration at Delhi Safdarjung Station, welcome drink, garland greeting.

Evening: Board Maharajas' Express and meet your butler. Dinner on board as the train journeys to Agra.

Overnight aboard train at Agra
Dinner Included

Day 2: Agra – Sunrise Taj Mahal & Agra Fort

Early Morning: Sunrise at the Taj Mahal, champagne breakfast at Taj Khema.

Afternoon: Tour of Agra Fort. Train departs for Sawai Madhopur.

Overnight aboard train at Sawai Madhopur
Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Day 3: Ranthambore & Jaipur – Tiger Safari & Palace Dinner

Early Morning: Wildlife game drive in Ranthambore Tiger Reserve.

Afternoon: Amber Fort excursion in Jaipur followed by a gala royal dinner at a heritage palace hotel.

Overnight aboard train to Bikaner
Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Day 4: Bikaner – Junagarh & Desert Dunes Barbecue

Morning: Explore Junagarh Fort in Bikaner.

Evening: Camel cart procession into Thar Desert dunes with folk dance performance and barbecue dinner under the stars.

Overnight aboard train to Jodhpur
Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Day 5: Jodhpur – Mehrangarh Fort & Royal Dinner

Morning: Mehrangarh Fort exploration and Jaswant Thada.

Evening: Royal cocktail and dinner at Hanwant Mahal overlooking illuminated Umaid Bhawan Palace.

Overnight aboard train to Udaipur
Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Day 6: Udaipur – Lake Pichola Boat Cruise & City Palace

Morning: Private boat cruise across Lake Pichola and City Palace tour.

Afternoon: Royal lunch at Fateh Prakash Palace. Farewell dinner on board.

Overnight aboard train to Mumbai
Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Day 7: Mumbai – Deboarding at CSMT

Morning: Farewell breakfast as the train arrives at Mumbai CSMT (approx 08:30 hrs).

Afternoon: Disembark with unforgettable memories. KoiKoi private transfers to hotel or airport.

HELPFUL FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions · The Indian Splendour

Everything you need to know about departures, excursions, dining, and reservations for this circuit.

Where does The Indian Splendour begin and conclude?
The tour departs from Safdarjung Railway Station in Delhi on scheduled Sunday afternoons, travels through Rajasthan, and concludes at Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CSMT) in Mumbai.
What are the highlights of the Agra visit on this route?
An early morning sunrise viewing of the Taj Mahal to enjoy its beauty before public crowds arrive, followed by a champagne breakfast at Taj Khema and a guided tour of Agra Fort.
How is Udaipur explored on Day 6?
Guests enjoy a private boat cruise across Lake Pichola, visit the grand City Palace and Crystal Gallery, and savor a royal lunch at Fateh Prakash Palace.
Can solo travelers book this circuit?
Yes, single supplement rates are available for all 4 cabin categories, providing private occupancy with all meals, excursions, and butler services included.
What is the booking deposit for The Indian Splendour?
A 25% deposit is required at booking, with the remaining 75% balance due 60 days before departure.
