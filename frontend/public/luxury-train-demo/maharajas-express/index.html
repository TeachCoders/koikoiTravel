Frequently Asked Questions · Maharajas' Express

Everything you need to know about cabins, dining, schedules, and reservations.

Where do Maharajas' Express journeys begin and conclude?
Departures depend on your chosen circuit:
  • The Heritage of India (7 Days): Begins at Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CSMT), Mumbai and concludes at Safdarjung Railway Station, Delhi.
  • The Indian Panorama (7 Days): Round-trip starting and ending at Safdarjung Railway Station, Delhi.
  • The Indian Splendour (7 Days): Starts at Safdarjung Railway Station, Delhi and concludes at CSMT, Mumbai.
  • Treasures of India (4 Days): Round-trip starting and ending at Safdarjung Railway Station, Delhi.
All guests are welcomed at the station lounge with a traditional red-carpet ceremonial welcome and briefing before boarding.
What is included in the Maharajas' Express ticket price?

Your ticket is fully inclusive: air-conditioned accommodation with private en-suite bathroom, all gourmet meals at Rang Mahal and Mayur Mahal, complimentary house wines, beers, and spirits, round-the-clock tea, coffee, and bottled water, 24/7 dedicated personal butler (Khidmatgar) service, guided off-train sightseeing in deluxe coaches, all monument admission tickets, standard still-camera fees, Ranthambore wildlife safari permits, and traditional cultural performances. Suite and Presidential Suite guests also enjoy private luxury cars and dedicated guides for all excursions.

What costs are not included in the fare?

Items outside the ticket fare include: 5% Government GST, optional off-train excursions (such as hotel spa treatments), professional video/filming permits at monuments, premium spirits or champagnes beyond the house pour selection, telephone calls, laundry services, and personal gratuities for tour guides and train staff.

What are the differences between Deluxe Cabins, Junior Suites, Suites, and the Presidential Suite?
Maharajas' Express offers 4 cabin categories:
  • Deluxe Cabin (112 sq. ft.): Available with twin or double beds, equipped with private bathroom, shower stall, electronic safe, wardrobe, and live television.
  • Junior Suite (150 sq. ft.): Features expansive picture windows, a separate writing desk, and twin or double bed layouts.
  • Suite (220 sq. ft.): Includes a separate living salon, private bedroom, full en-suite bathtub, mini-bar, and a complimentary private luxury car with exclusive guide during all excursions.
  • Presidential Suite 'Navratna' (448 sq. ft.): Spans an entire railway carriage featuring two full bedrooms, two bathrooms (one with bathtub), living area, private dining salon, personal valet, and dedicated private car throughout the journey.
Can vegetarian, Jain, vegan, or special diets be accommodated?

Yes, with ease. The onboard chefs prepare fresh meals for all dietary preferences. Pure vegetarian, Jain (prepared without onion or garlic), vegan, gluten-free, lactose-free, and Halal meals are carefully arranged. Please share any dietary restrictions with us when booking so the onboard culinary team can prepare in advance.

What is the recommended dress code?

During daytime excursions, monument visits, and the Ranthambore wildlife safari, smart-casual and comfortable lightweight clothing is ideal, paired with sturdy walking shoes and sun protection. In the evenings, dinners at Rang Mahal and Mayur Mahal welcome smart-casual or semi-formal dress. For special theme evenings and palace dinners, guests often enjoy wearing festive traditional Indian or formal attire.

How is luggage managed inside the cabins?

Each cabin has designated storage space under the beds and overhead racks, best suited for medium soft-sided suitcases or standard trolley bags. If you have extra or oversized baggage, it can be safely stored in the train's secure luggage van throughout the trip at no extra charge.
