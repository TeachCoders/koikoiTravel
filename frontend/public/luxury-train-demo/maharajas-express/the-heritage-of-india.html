OVERVIEW

About The Heritage of India

Departing from India's commercial capital Mumbai, this flagship journey winds through royal Rajasthan's lake palaces, desert citadels, and tiger reserves, before reaching the Taj Mahal and capital city Delhi. Experience 7 days of unparalleled IRCTC luxury aboard the World's Leading Luxury Train.
DETAILED PLAN

The Heritage of India Itinerary

Day 1: Mumbai – Welcome Ceremony & Boarding at CSMT

Afternoon: Registration and check-in at the magnificent Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CSMT), Mumbai. Experience a traditional Indian welcome with fragrant marigold garlands, royal shehnai musicians, and welcome refreshments.

Evening: Board Maharajas' Express and meet your personal dedicated butler. Settle into your handcrafted cabin as the train departs Mumbai and journeys northeast into royal Rajasthan. Savor your inaugural royal dinner at Rang Mahal restaurant.

Overnight aboard Maharajas' Express en route to Udaipur
Dinner Included

Day 2: Udaipur – Lake Pichola Boat Cruise & City Palace

Morning: Wake up in the romantic City of Lakes. Following breakfast on board, embark on a private motorboat cruise across the tranquil waters of Lake Pichola with majestic views of Lake Palace and Jag Mandir.

Afternoon: Guided VIP tour of Udaipur City Palace and the rare Crystal Gallery. Enjoy a lavish lunch at the Satkar Hall of Fateh Prakash Palace or on board. Spend the late afternoon at leisure exploring local craft bazaars.

Overnight aboard Maharajas' Express departing for Jodhpur
Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Day 3: Jodhpur – Mehrangarh Fort & Royal Gala at Hanwant Mahal

Morning: Arrive in the Blue City of Jodhpur. Explore the impregnable Mehrangarh Fort towering 400 feet above the skyline, followed by the gleaming white marble cenotaphs of Jaswant Thada.

Evening: Private royal cocktail reception and Marwari banquet dinner at Hanwant Mahal overlooking illuminated Umaid Bhawan Palace, accompanied by Kalbeliya and Ghoomar folk performances.

Overnight aboard Maharajas' Express departing for Bikaner
Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Day 4: Bikaner – Junagarh Fort & Thar Sand Dunes Banquet

Morning: Arrive in Bikaner, city of desert warriors. Tour the unvanquished Junagarh Fort with its opulent Anup Mahal and Badal Mahal painted in gold leaf.

Evening: Journey via camel carts into the undulating Thar Desert sand dunes. Sip champagne as the sun sinks beneath the horizon, followed by a lavish open-air barbecue banquet under starry skies with fiery Langa folk songs.

Overnight aboard Maharajas' Express to Jaipur
Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Day 5: Jaipur – The Pink City, Amber Fort & Royal Lunch

Morning: Arrive in Jaipur, Rajasthan's Pink City. Ascent to the hilltop Amber Fort via private royal jeeps. Marvel at the intricate mirror mosaics of Sheesh Mahal.

Afternoon: Exclusive royal lunch at a heritage palace hotel. Visit the UNESCO Jantar Mantar observatory and drive past the iconic honeycombed facade of Hawa Mahal.

Overnight aboard train to Sawai Madhopur
Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Day 6: Ranthambore Tiger Safari & UNESCO Fatehpur Sikri

Early Morning: Embark on an early morning open-top jungle game drive in Ranthambore National Park in search of Bengal tigers, leopards, marsh crocodiles, and sambar deer.

Afternoon: Tour Emperor Akbar's abandoned 16th-century red sandstone imperial capital at Fatehpur Sikri, including Buland Darwaza and Panch Mahal.

Overnight aboard Maharajas' Express parked at Agra
Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Day 7: Agra & Delhi – Sunrise Taj Mahal & Farewell at Delhi

Early Morning: Unforgettable sunrise visit to the Taj Mahal as the first rays of sunlight illuminate the white marble dome. Followed by champagne breakfast at Taj Khema.

Afternoon: Visit the red sandstone fortress of Agra Fort. Savor a farewell lunch on board as the train returns to Delhi Safdarjung Station (approx 15:30 hrs). Disembark with royal farewell mementos.

Tour Concludes in Delhi · Airport Transfers

HELPFUL FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions · The Heritage of India

Everything you need to know about departures, excursions, dining, and reservations for this circuit.

Where does The Heritage of India journey depart from and terminate?
This journey departs from Mumbai at Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CSMT) on scheduled Sunday afternoons and concludes 7 days later at Delhi Safdarjung Railway Station.
Is the Ranthambore tiger safari included on this route?
Yes, an early morning open-top jungle game drive in Ranthambore National Park with experienced naturalists and park permits is fully included in your ticket.
What exclusive royal banquets are included in this journey?
Guests enjoy a private royal cocktail reception and Marwari banquet dinner at Hanwant Mahal overlooking illuminated Umaid Bhawan Palace in Jodhpur, and a gala barbecue feast with folk music on the Thar Desert sand dunes in Bikaner.
What is the difference between Deluxe Cabin and Suite categories on this train?
Deluxe Cabins (112 sq. ft.) offer twin or double beds with attached en-suite shower bathrooms and shared group excursions in AC coaches. Suites (220 sq. ft.) provide a separate living room, bedroom, full bathtub, and a complimentary private luxury car with exclusive guide during all excursions.
How do I book The Heritage of India and what deposit is required?
An advance deposit of 25% of the fare secures your reservation. The remaining 75% balance is payable 60 days before your departure. KoiKoi Travel assists with IRCTC seat allocation, pre/post luxury hotels in Mumbai or Delhi, and airport transfers.
