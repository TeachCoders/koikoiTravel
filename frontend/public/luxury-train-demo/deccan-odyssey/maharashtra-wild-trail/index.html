OVERVIEW

About Maharashtra Wild Trail

Designed for wildlife enthusiasts and heritage travelers, the Maharashtra Wild Trail pairs two of India's most celebrated tiger reserves—Tadoba-Andhari and Pench—with the monumental rock-cut art of Ellora and Ajanta.

Track apex predators on open-top jeep safaris through dense teak forests by day, and return each evening to 5-star comfort, gourmet dining, and royal hospitality onboard the blue limousine of Indian rails.

DETAILED PLAN

Maharashtra Wild Trail Itinerary

Day 1: Boarding at Mumbai (CSMT) & Departure toward Marathwada

Board the royal blue carriages of Deccan Odyssey at Mumbai CSMT by 15:30 hrs. Receive a traditional Maharashtrian welcome with garlands, tutari horns, and sweet offerings.

Settle into your deluxe cabin as the train departs Mumbai, climbing through the Western Ghats tunnels toward Aurangabad. Enjoy dinner onboard in the Waavar restaurant.

Onboard Deccan Odyssey toward Aurangabad
Dinner onboard

Day 2: Aurangabad – Kailash Temple at Ellora Caves & Bibi Ka Maqbara

Morning arrival in Aurangabad. Disembark after breakfast to explore the UNESCO World Heritage Ellora Caves, focusing on Cave 16: the breathtaking monolithic Kailash Temple carved from top to bottom out of a single rock cliff.

Return to the train for lunch, followed by an afternoon visit to Bibi Ka Maqbara, the graceful 17th-century mausoleum built by Prince Azam Shah, and explore local workshops weaving traditional gold-bordered Paithani silk sarees.

Onboard Deccan Odyssey toward Ramtek
Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner onboard

Day 3: Aurangabad & Daulatabad Hill Fortress

Today explore the impregnable medieval hill citadel of Daulatabad (Devgiri), rising dramatically on a conical hill. Walk across the moat and through the ingenious dark labyrinth ('Andhari') designed to confuse invading armies.

Return to the Deccan Odyssey for an afternoon of leisure, spa treatments in the Ayush wellness car, and scenic cruising through the heart of the Deccan plateau toward Central India's tiger country.

Onboard Deccan Odyssey toward Ramtek (Pench)
Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner onboard

Day 4: Ramtek – Morning & Afternoon Safaris in Pench Tiger Reserve

Arrive at Ramtek station, gateway to Pench National Park—the legendary jungle setting of Rudyard Kipling's *The Jungle Book*. Board open 4x4 safari vehicles for an early morning game drive through teak and bamboo forests.

Search for Royal Bengal tigers, leopards, wild dogs (dhole), gaur (Indian bison), and spotted deer. After lunch at a luxury jungle resort, embark on a late afternoon safari to catch predators on the prowl at sunset.

Onboard Deccan Odyssey toward Warora
Breakfast onboard · Resort Lunch in Pench · Dinner onboard

Day 5: Warora – Twin Safaris in Tadoba-Andhari Tiger Reserve

Arrive in Warora for a full day inside Tadoba-Andhari Tiger Reserve, Maharashtra's oldest, largest, and most tiger-dense national park. Known as 'The Land of the Tigers', Tadoba offers some of the highest tiger-sighting probabilities in the world.

Enjoy both morning and afternoon game drives through the Moharli and Kolara zones, tracking tigers near waterholes, observing sloth bears foraging for termites, and spotting marsh crocodiles in Tadoba Lake. Return to the train for evening celebrations.

Onboard Deccan Odyssey toward Jalgaon
Breakfast onboard · Safari Lodge Lunch · Dinner onboard

Day 6: Jalgaon – Ancient Buddhist Caves of Ajanta

Disembark at Jalgaon for the UNESCO World Heritage site of Ajanta Caves. Explore the 30 rock-cut Buddhist chaityas and viharas perched above the scenic river bend.

Marvel at the remarkably preserved 2nd-century BCE mural paintings narrating Jataka tales of Buddha's previous lives. Return to the train for afternoon high tea and relaxation.

Onboard Deccan Odyssey toward Nashik
Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner onboard

Day 7: Nashik – Godavari Temple Ghats & Sula Vineyards Wine Tasting

Arrive in Nashik, Maharashtra's holy river city and premier wine valley. Visit the sacred Ramkund ghats on the Godavari River and the historic black-stone Kalaram Temple.

In the afternoon, tour Sula or Grover Zampa Vineyards for an exclusive barrel-room tasting of fine Indian wines paired with lunch. Reboard Deccan Odyssey for your farewell gala dinner as the train journeys back to Mumbai.

Onboard Deccan Odyssey heading toward Mumbai
Breakfast onboard · Vineyard Lunch · Farewell Gala Dinner

Day 8: Arrival at Mumbai (CSMT) & Farewell

Arrive back at Mumbai CSMT around 08:15 hrs. Enjoy a hearty farewell breakfast onboard before concluding your wilderness and heritage expedition through Maharashtra.

Disembark with personalized assistance from your train crew. Chauffeur transfers to Mumbai airports or hotels are arranged by KoiKoi Travel.

Frequently Asked Questions · Maharashtra Wild Trail

Everything you need to know about departures, excursions, dining, and reservations for this circuit.

What is the difference between Deluxe Cabin and Presidential Suite on Deccan Odyssey?
A Deluxe Cabin measures approximately 95 sq. ft. and features twin single beds or a double bed, private ensuite bathroom with hot shower, electronic safe, individual climate control, and a dedicated 24/7 personal valet. A Presidential Suite spans an expansive 205 sq. ft., occupying an entire carriage section with a separate living lounge, master bedroom, two private bathrooms, and complimentary minibar.
What is the booking deposit and cancellation policy?
To confirm your reservation, a 25% advance deposit is payable at the time of booking. The remaining 75% balance is due 60 days prior to train departure. If booking within 60 days of travel, 100% full payment is required. Standard IRCTC/MTDC cancellation terms apply.
How does dining work onboard Deccan Odyssey?
Deccan Odyssey houses two beautifully decorated fine-dining restaurants: Waavar and Utsav. Both offer rotating menus prepared fresh daily by onboard executive chefs, featuring authentic regional Indian specialties (Maharashtrian, Goan, Rajasthani, Mughlai) as well as international continental gourmet dishes.
Can KoiKoi Travel arrange hotel stays in Mumbai or Delhi before and after the train?
Yes, absolutely. We arrange complete 5-star heritage extensions: booking luxury stays at The Taj Mahal Palace in Mumbai or The Imperial in New Delhi, private chauffeured airport transfers, and private guided city tours tailored to your schedule.
What dress code is recommended for off-train excursions?
Smart casual, lightweight cotton clothing and comfortable walking shoes are ideal for daytime temple, fort, and cave explorations. When visiting sacred temples and mosques, shoulders and knees must be respectfully covered. For evening dinners onboard and palace events, smart elegant attire is welcomed.
