DECCAN ODYSSEY · ITINERARIES

6 Distinct 7 Nights / 8 Days Circuits

Covering Western, Central, and Northern India from Mumbai and Delhi.

Circuit 1 · 7 Nights / 8 Days

Maharashtra Splendour (Mumbai to Mumbai)

Route: Mumbai → Nashik → Aurangabad (Ellora Caves) → Ajanta Caves → Kolhapur → Goa → Sindhudurg → Mumbai.
Highlights include UNESCO Ellora rock-cut temples, Nashik wine tasting, Kolhapur martial arts, and Goa beach gala.

Circuit 2 · 7 Nights / 8 Days

Indian Odyssey (Delhi to Mumbai)

Route: Delhi → Sawai Madhopur → Agra → Jaipur → Udaipur → Vadodara → Ellora Caves → Mumbai.
Highlights include Taj Mahal, Ranthambore tiger safari, Amber Fort, Laxmi Vilas Palace and Kailasa temple.

Circuit 3 · 7 Nights / 8 Days

Maharashtra Wild Trail (Mumbai to Mumbai)

Route: Mumbai → Aurangabad → Pench National Park → Tadoba Tiger Reserve → Ajanta → Nashik → Mumbai.
Twin tiger reserve safaris in Mowgli's jungle (Pench) and Tadoba along with ancient Buddhist fresco caves.

Private customized and small-group tours across India, planned by a small travel team.
