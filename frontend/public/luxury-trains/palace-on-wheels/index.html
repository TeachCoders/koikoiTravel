PALACE ON WHEELS · OVERVIEW

The Pioneer of Luxury Rail Travel in India

Step aboard the authentic coaches inspired by erstwhile Rajput rulers and the British Viceroy. An unforgettable 7 Nights / 8 Days circular journey starting and finishing in Delhi.

View Itinerary Check Departure Dates
The Royal Experience

A Week Across the Land of Kings

Palace on Wheels was rated the 4th best luxury train in the world by Condé Nast. Each saloon is named after princely states of Rajasthan, furnished in royal heritage decor with private modern bathrooms.

14 Deluxe Saloons

Named after Mewar, Marwar, Bikaner, Amber, and other princely states, with two private Khidmatgars per coach.

2 Fine Dining Cars

Sheesh Mahal and Swarn Mahal serve authentic Rajasthani, Mughlai, Continental and Chinese delicacies.

The Bar & Lounge

Relax with fine scotches, Indian wines and signature cocktails in a vintage regal setting with scenic viewing windows.

Royal Rejuvenation Spa

Dedicated Ayurvedic wellness coach offering reflexology, herbal therapies and massages by certified therapists.
