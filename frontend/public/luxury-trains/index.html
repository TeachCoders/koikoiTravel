Luxury Train Journeys in India: Handcrafted Royal Expeditions

Experience the Subcontinent Aboard India's 4 Iconic Palaces on Wheels, Planned by Independent Travel Specialists

Stepping aboard one of India's legendary luxury trains is a journey back to the golden era of rail travel. Far beyond ordinary transit, each voyage is an immersive five-star sanctuary where panoramic observation windows frame Rajput hill forts, untamed wildlife sanctuaries, and golden desert dunes—accompanied by white-gloved butler service, fine dining salons, and curated private excursions.

Independent Luxury Travel Curator & Advisory Notice KoiKoi Travel is an independent bespoke luxury tour designer. India's four luxury trains are operated by their respective state rail corporations and tourism boards (IRCTC, RTDC, KSTDC, and MTDC). While train operators provide only station-to-station rail tickets, KoiKoi Travel crafts the complete, seamless vacation around your train voyage: securing your preferred cabin class, managing departure logistics, providing private chauffeured luxury transfers, arranging handpicked 5-star heritage palace stays in Delhi and Mumbai, and designing private guided pre- and post-rail excursions.

Choosing between India's four legendary trains depends on your travel season, preferred pace, and the regions you wish to discover:

  • Palace on Wheels (7 Nights / 8 Days): India's original luxury train (operating since 1982). A classic circuit through the royal heart of Rajasthan—Jaipur's Pink City, Ranthambore tiger safari, Chittorgarh fort, Udaipur's lakes, Jaisalmer's Thar desert dunes, and Jodhpur—culminating at Agra's Taj Mahal. Ideal for first-time visitors seeking timeless Rajputana romance.
  • Maharajas' Express (3 to 6 Nights): India's most modern and opulent luxury train, featuring pneumatic suspension, twin fine-dining restaurants (Mayur Mahal & Rang Mahal), and Presidential Suites spanning entire carriages. Offers four distinct routes connecting Delhi, Agra, and Rajasthan with the temple carvings of Khajuraho and the sacred ghats of Varanasi.
  • The Golden Chariot (3 to 5 Nights): South India's premier luxury train gliding from Bengaluru through the boulder-strewn UNESCO World Heritage ruins of Hampi, Mysore's ornate palaces, Bandipur tiger reserve, and the sunlit beaches of Goa. Best for travelers seeking southern architecture, culture, and nature.
  • Deccan Odyssey (7 Nights / 8 Days): The royal blue train of Western India, navigating Maharashtra, Gujarat, and Rajasthan. Highlights include the awe-inspiring rock-cut UNESCO caves of Ajanta and Ellora, Nashik's vineyard valleys, and vibrant coastal heritage.

FAQs

Luxury Train Questions

Which is the best luxury train in India?

It depends on what you want to see. Palace on Wheels is best for Rajasthan and the Taj Mahal. Maharajas' Express has the most journey choices. Golden Chariot covers South India. Deccan Odyssey mixes Maharashtra, Rajasthan and Gujarat. Message us and we will suggest one.

When do the luxury trains run?

Most run in the cooler months, roughly September or October to March or April. Exact departure dates change every season, so each train page lists the current dates.

How much does a luxury train journey cost?

The price depends on the train, journey, cabin type and travel dates. We do not publish a fixed price here. Send us your dates and we will share a clear quote.

What is included on board?

In general, your cabin, meals, sightseeing with a guide and on-board service are part of the journey. Inclusions differ by train, so please check the train page or ask us for the exact list.

Can you add hotels, flights or a private tour?

Yes. We can add a hotel before or after your train, airport transfers, domestic flights and a private tour to any other part of India.

Can children travel on these trains?

Rules are different for each train. Palace on Wheels, for example, treats guests up to 12 years as children. Tell us who is travelling and we will confirm the policy for your train.
