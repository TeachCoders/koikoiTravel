## 2. Key Travel Regions & Circuits

### A. The Golden Triangle (Delhi - Agra - Jaipur)
- **Overview**: India's most famous introductory travel circuit, connecting the capital city of Delhi, the Mughal splendor of Agra (Taj Mahal), and the Pink City of Jaipur.
- **Recommended Duration**: 4 to 8 Days.
- **Key Attractions**:
  - Delhi: Qutub Minar, Humayun's Tomb, India Gate, Chandni Chowk rickshaw ride.
  - Agra: Sunrise at the Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, Fatehpur Sikri.
  - Jaipur: Amber Fort, City Palace, Hawa Mahal (Palace of Winds), Jantar Mantar observatory.
- **Extensions**: Ranthambore National Park (Tiger Safari), Varanasi (Ganga Aarti), Udaipur (Lake City), or Rishikesh (Yoga & Ganges).

### B. Rajasthan (The Land of Kings)
- **Overview**: Majestic desert state famous for royal forts, palatial heritage hotels, and desert culture.
- **Major Hubs**:
  - **Jaipur**: Royal architecture, gems, textiles, and authentic Rajasthani cuisine.
  - **Udaipur**: Romantic lake city, Lake Pichola boat cruises, Jag Mandir, and City Palace.
  - **Jodhpur**: The Blue City dominated by the colossal Mehrangarh Fort.
  - **Jaisalmer**: The Golden Fort, Thar Desert camel safaris, and luxury dune camping.
  - **Pushkar**: Sacred lake, Brahma Temple, and vibrant annual camel fair.

### C. Kerala (God's Own Country)
- **Overview**: Tropical southern paradise celebrated for calm palm-fringed backwaters, misty tea plantations, spice gardens, and authentic Ayurveda wellness.
- **Major Hubs**:
  - **Munnar**: Rolling emerald tea estates, Eravikulam National Park (Nilgiri Tahr).
  - **Thekkady**: Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary, spice plantation walks, and bamboo rafting.
  - **Alleppey / Kumarakom**: Overnight luxury houseboat (Kettuvallam) cruises with private chef.
  - **Kochi**: Fort Kochi, Chinese fishing nets, Jewish Synagogue, and Kathakali performances.
  - **Kovalam & Varkala**: Cliff-top beaches, Arabian Sea sunsets, and Ayurvedic spa therapies.

### D. Kashmir & Ladakh (The Trans-Himalayas)
- **Overview**: Snow-capped peaks, tranquil alpine lakes, and dramatic high-altitude moonscapes.
- **Kashmir**: Srinagar Dal Lake Shikara rides, Gulmarg Gondola (highest cable car), Pahalgam Betaab Valley, and Sonamarg glaciers.
- **Ladakh**: Leh Palace, Pangong Tso Lake (bordering Tibet), Nubra Valley double-humped camel rides via Khardung La (one of the world's highest motorable roads).

### E. Himachal Pradesh & Uttarakhand (Himalayan Retreats)
- **Shimla & Manali**: Colonial heritage, Solang Valley adventure sports, and Atal Tunnel to Sissu.
- **Rishikesh & Haridwar**: Global yoga capital, holy Ganga Aarti at Triveni Ghat, and white-water river rafting.
